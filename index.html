Breath
on Glass

Fog up your own reflection, then write in it with a fingertip.

Nothing is uploaded. Your camera and microphone are processed right here in this browser tab. No frames, audio, or data ever leave your machine.

breathe / trace / clear

  • 01Breathe

    Blow softly at your laptop. Fog blooms right where your breath lands.

  • 02Trace

    Raise only your index finger and move it like a pen. The fingertip clears a smooth path through the fog.

  • 03Fist

    Close your hand and swipe to clear a wide patch, like using your sleeve.
